Quiet, Please

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

Quiet, Please is a single-player library game. It does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the game stores

Your progress (the current day, coins, upgrades, records and achievements) and your settings (sound, music and graphics) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or personal details.
  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or third-party tracking tools.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • No in-app purchases.

Game updates

When you open the game, it checks GitHub Pages for a newer version of the game files and downloads it if one exists. This request contains no personal information. Like any website, GitHub may record standard technical details such as your IP address in its server logs, under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or use that information.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
