Common questions

How do I play?

Tap where you want to walk, then tap the desk, a shelf or a patron to act. Check books in and out at the desk, carry returns to the matching section, and shush noisy patrons before the noise meter fills. Finish with a high enough rating to pass the day.

Can I use a controller or keyboard?

Yes. Pair a Bluetooth controller or keyboard with your iPhone or iPad. Left stick or WASD to walk, A or E to interact, B or Esc to go back.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Deleting the app erases your progress. To start the week again, use Start over from Monday on the day card.

The game says an update is ready.

New game content downloads in the background. Tap Update ready · Restart between shifts, or just close and reopen the app.
